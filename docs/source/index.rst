Relay Orbiter
=============

**Deploy a relay constellation with one launch and one button.**

Relay Orbiter is a Kerbal Space Program 1.12 mod. It takes a carrier vessel holding 2
to 12 relay satellites and puts them on a shared circular orbit, evenly spaced: it
reaches the orbit you choose (optionally launching from the pad with MechJeb),
releases one satellite per parking orbit, switches to each one, circularizes it and
comes back to the carrier.
