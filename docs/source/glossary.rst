Glossary
========

.. glossary::

   Carrier
      The vessel that holds the satellites and releases them.

   Parking orbit
      The carrier's orbit during the deployment, whose period is (N−1)/N (inner) or
      (N+1)/N (outer) of the satellites' period.

   Inner / outer parking orbit
      Inner: below the target orbit, satellites released at its apoapsis. Outer: above
      it, satellites released at its periapsis.

   Release point
      Where the parking orbit touches the target orbit: the apoapsis (inner) or periapsis
      (outer).

   Separator
      The decoupler or docking port that releases a satellite.

   Synchronous orbit
      The circular orbit whose period equals the body's rotation period.

   Hohmann transfer
      Two burns between circular orbits: one to reach the other altitude, one to
      circularize there.

   Ascending / descending node
      Where the orbit crosses the body's equator going north / south; plane changes
      happen there.

   SOI
      Sphere of influence: beyond it the body's gravity no longer dominates.

   SAS level
      What a probe core or pilot can point at: 0 stability only, 1 prograde/retrograde,
      2 also normal/radial, 3 also target and maneuver node.

   Packed / on rails
      A vessel under time warp or far from the active vessel, moved along its orbit
      without physics.

   Load range
      How far from the active vessel KSP keeps vessels loaded (2.5 km in orbit by
      default); switching to an unloaded vessel reloads the scene.

   Delta-v (Δv)
      Change in velocity a burn produces, or a vessel can produce with its fuel.
